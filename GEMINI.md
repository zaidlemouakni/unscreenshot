# unscreenshot

When the user shares an image of a slide (screenshot, PNG, JPG, WebP, PDF page) and wants it recreated, made editable, or converted to PowerPoint, follow `skills/unscreenshot/SKILL.md` step by step. Paths are relative to this extension's folder (`~/.gemini/extensions/unscreenshot/`, or `~/.qwen/extensions/unscreenshot/` in Qwen Code). The scripts are in `skills/unscreenshot/scripts/` and need Python 3 with Pillow and numpy.
